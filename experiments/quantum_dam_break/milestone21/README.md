# M21 — Pressure-based high-density-ratio dam release

**Scope:** advances the one integrated D3Q19/D3Q7 dam-break architecture. Experimental hybrid QLBM, no QPU, no quantum advantage. M20 retained intact. The phase variable is now alpha in [0,1], not signed phi. Material density and dynamic viscosity enter the actual solver, not merely the output. The physical density and viscosity ratios are representative of water/air; the lattice parameters are not a calibrated SI experiment.

## Model

Following the velocity-based hydrodynamic LBE of Fakhari et al., PRE 96, 053301 (2017), use pressure populations f whose zeroth moment P=p/(rho cs²) and first moment (with half-force shift) represent velocity, NOT density or physical momentum. cs²=1/3. rho=1/R+(1-1/R)alpha and mu=nu_liquid*(1/MuRatio+(1-1/MuRatio)alpha), nu=mu/rho. R=998/1.2, MuRatio=.001/.000018, nu_liquid=.02 lu. The hydro equilibrium is w_i[P+3 c_i·u+4.5(c_i·u)^2-1.5|u|²]. The five deviatoric stress modes use s_nu=1/(.5+nu/cs²); bulk rate 1, higher kinetic rates 1.2. Conserved pressure and velocity modes have zero rate, and the source updates the velocity modes. The pressure-force options are the paper's -cs² P grad(rho)/rho, or our alternative cs² grad(P)-grad(p)/rho (same continuous product rule, different discrete operators). The latter is used in the release experiment. Gravity is a constant acceleration. Chemical potential is 48 sigma/xi alpha(alpha-1)(alpha-.5)-1.5 sigma xi lap(alpha); capillary acceleration = mu_chem grad(alpha)/rho. Viscous density-gradient correction nu(grad u+grad u^T)grad(rho)/rho uses an explicit force-corrected predictor, not the paper's local stress recovery. D3Q19 isotropic centered stencils with edge ghost values; stationary halfway bounce-back at all outer faces. D3Q7 phase equilibrium is first-order in velocity, phase source is w_i c_i·n 4 alpha(1-alpha)/xi with trapezoidal source treatment. Existing M20 signed limiter is applied by transforming phase populations h to 2h-w, then back, preserving the local zeroth moment. This is an adaptation, not a reproduction of the publication's D2Q9 MRT discretization.

## Quantum and classical separation

Four-qubit dual-rail damping is executed as controlled RY rotations, CNOT and conditional rail SWAP on complex amplitudes, with variable angles computed classically from local viscosity. Test verifies 128 different rates and signed moment inputs against the existing independent 16x16 unitary. The phase channel uses M19 gate implementation. D3Q19 and D3Q7 streaming/reflection use M18 compiled fully controlled-X networks on dense complex statevectors; 16 and 14 qubits, 254808 and 62144 logical MCX respectively per large-grid timestep. Exact expectations and signed amplitude readout are simulated; no finite-shot experiment at the new density ratio, no native Qiskit (unavailable), and no coherent end-to-end timestep. Moment transforms, pressure and nonlinear equilibrium, material interpolation, phase limiter, gradients, force and reconstruction are classical. For 96 steps on 2048 cells there are 4,128,768 local dissipative channel invocations (15+6 nonconserved modes per cell per step); not one small universal circuit or a hardware shot count. State prep/readout and MCX decomposition costs not included.

## Reproduction and results

Run `bash milestone21/reproduce.sh` from the experiment root. `--full-regression` reruns historical tests. Output histories [time, field, z,y,x], field order alpha,rho,mu,p,ux,uy,uz. Each VTI contains physical lattice pressure, material density, dynamic viscosity, fraction and force-corrected velocity. All seven snapshots are computed, not generated graphics. No SI conversion. `run.py` guards maximum speed >0.1 lu; records the failed case, not silently clips it. Tests use separate population-space MRT algebra and slice push-stream, scalar limiter; they share physical source/equilibrium formulae. Hydrostatic pressure from piecewise-constant density integration is independent physical check with frozen phase, not a test of dynamic interface equilibrium.

## Limits / acceptance gates

This is a preliminary high-density-ratio velocity-based LBM with classical coupling, not a completed water/air solver. Global interpolated mass conservation follows from alpha conservation and linear material interpolation; does not prove local continuity, consistency of phase diffusion and momentum flux, or conservation of physical momentum in a variable-density flow. Pressure population sum is NOT material mass. No Laplace pressure, two-layer Poiseuille or experimental front-height benchmark yet. Need gauge-invariant/well-balanced pressure discretization, local density/momentum flux consistency, resolved-interface refinement, gravity and viscosity scaling, static droplets, physical Reynolds/Bond/Weber matching before realistic water-air validation. Surface tension included but not calibrated. To move toward genuine quantum simulation, nonlinear force/material/limiter and equilibrium computation must be quantum-implemented or explicitly scoped as hybrid. Full real QPU remains blocked by resource/preparation/readout costs. No remote GitHub changes.
