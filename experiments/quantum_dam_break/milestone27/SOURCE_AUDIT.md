# Scientific and source audit

- Baseline: complete Milestone 26 archive (same M23 pressure/force equations, M25 nominal Re=212 scaling). M24 spanwise-periodic transport restricted to span-uniform fields. M27 instead uses closed walls on all six faces, consistent with the existing zero-normal-derivative stencils and conservative phase flux limiter. No new governing equation is claimed.
- Fakhari, Mitchell, Leonardi and Bolster (2017), *Improved locality of the phase-field lattice-Boltzmann model for immiscible fluids at high density ratios*, Physical Review E 96, 053301, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.96.053301. Relevant pressure/velocity and high-density-ratio reference; the M23/M27 model is an independent adaptation, not a reproduction.
- *Experimental Investigations of Partial-Breach Dam-Break Flows* (2016), https://doi.org/10.1061/(ASCE)HY.1943-7900.0001185. Reports three-dimensional flow field, lateral and downstream wave propagation. Motivates measuring both directions, not a matched experimental geometry.
- Lobovský et al. (2014), *Experimental investigation of dynamic pressure loads during dam break*, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0889974614000656. Motivates separate wetting and pressure diagnostics; no experimental validation claimed.
- Remote repository aswanthbajith/QLBM, branch research/quantum-waterfall-impact-vqc, checked head 9794b4525eb08f0683b101afcdf30db3fca550a5 (visualization). Not merged; no remote writes. Local baseline M26 source commit f284d9e.

The finite-width water block is an instantaneous release in a closed tank, not a partial-breach opening with modeled moving gate. The 8-cell reservoir width is a design choice, not a calibrated experimental geometry. M25 material ratio and nominal Re are retained; absolute water viscosity, contact angle, experimental pressure validation, Qiskit execution and hardware execution remain unresolved. The hydrostatic background remains a fixed classical correction. The two backends share the physical model and vectorized limiter; their collision algebra and streaming implementations differ. Independent scalar FCT is tested separately.
