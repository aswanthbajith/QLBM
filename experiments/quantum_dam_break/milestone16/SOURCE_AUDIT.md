`QuantumPhaseFieldPrototype.build_step_circuit` appends only the local 4-qubit block encoding; spatial streaming is a comment. `compute_chemical_potential` evaluates the cubic and Laplacian with NumPy. `run_quantum_dissipative_step` loops over local nodes and multiplies by the dilation matrix, rescales projected amplitudes and averages per-node probabilities. Its benchmark initializes `g_i=w_i phi_eq` then checks `sum_i g_i` after collision. Conservation of this prepared field during local collision is not a test of spatial Cahn–Hilliard evolution. The average of node probabilities is not the herald probability of a globally amplitude-encoded state unless node norms are equal; the correct global probability is norm-squared-weighted. The spatial flattening in `run_quantum_dissipative_step` is not used for the local loop. A separate global postselection/streaming audit remains necessary. The cited branch's other published results were not rerun here. No changes to these files were made.
