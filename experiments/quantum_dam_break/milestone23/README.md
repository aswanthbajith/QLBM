# Milestone 23: face-based pressure and density-gradient stabilization

**Target:** the integrated D3Q19/D3Q7 two-phase dam-break solver, not a new solver family. M22's frozen-interface hydrodynamic Jacobian had unstable eigenvalues at high density ratio. This milestone changes only the pressure correction and explicit relative-density-gradient viscous correction, retaining M22's fixed hydrostatic reference, M20 limiter and M21 quantum subroutines.

## Discretization
For a link i→j with lattice velocity c and weight w, let r=(rho_j-rho_i)/(rho_j+rho_i). The pressure correction is
`sum w*c*((P_j-P_i)-2*(rho_j*P_j-rho_i*P_i)/(rho_j+rho_i)) = -sum w*c*r*(P_j+P_i)`.
Here P=p_g/(rho*cs^2). The force uses the arithmetic mean of the two positive cell densities in the pressure acceleration denominator. This is NOT harmonic averaging of density. The relative density gradient used by the viscous correction is `sum w*c*2*r/cs^2`; for smooth positive rho it approximates grad(rho)/rho (second-order interior consistency). Edge extension is the same as M21. This bounds the link contrast but does not prove correct traction transmission at discontinuities, physical momentum conservation, or unconditional nonlinear stability. The fixed reference residual is still required for the exact stationary result. These are classical force calculations.

## Reproduction
From experiments/quantum_dam_break with the earlier environment installed, `bash milestone23/reproduce.sh` runs ablation, main experiments, gravity scan, plots and focused tests. `--full-regression` additionally runs the historical tests. The existing M22 results included in the archive are used by the plot comparison. `run.py` reuses the exact M22 harness in memory; it does not edit previous files. The reference collision/streaming is independent population-space code, but shares the new force equations; a separate scalar loop tests the face formulas. The Jacobian is finite-difference on the frozen-phase hydrodynamic map only, 608 population variables, threshold 1+1e-5, at eps 1e-8 and 1e-9. It is not a proof for coupled moving interfaces.

## Actual results
At density ratio 831.6667, all nine 64-step stationary/perturbed gate-subroutine runs at widths 0.1, 0.5 and 1.0 completed. At width 0.1 the 1e-5 perturbation decayed to 4.285e-7; 0.5 to 2.922e-7; 1.0 to 1.703e-7. All six new Jacobians have zero unstable modes; M22 had 17 and 2 for widths 0.1 and 0.5. Ablation shows either change alone insufficient at width 0.1. The 96-step gate dam-release trajectory (32x4x16, gravity .0005, sigma 1e-5) gives peak speed .049353, phase drift 8.583e-12, material-mass drift 8.527e-12, phase RMSE 3.953e-16 against the matched reference, population max difference 8.216e-15. Phase remains bounded within roundoff. Zero-gravity control has peak speed 5.576e-5. Classical gravity scan completes 96 steps at .00075 and .001; guard stops .0015 at 45, .002 at 5 and .003 at 3. The speed guard is a chosen numerical validity threshold, not proof of divergence. No SI calibration, experimental comparison or physical momentum budget is established. All new force calculations remain classical; previous ideal dual-rail dissipation gates and permutation streaming/reflection are retained. No QPU run or speedup claim. The next priorities are physical traction and momentum transport verification (two-layer flow, Laplace pressure), and careful gravity/time/space scaling. Do not infer physical accuracy from eigenvalue stability alone.
