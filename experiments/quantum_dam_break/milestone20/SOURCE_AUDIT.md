## Literature (metadata/abstracts verified in this turn)
- Zalesak, S.T. (1979), *Fully multidimensional flux-corrected transport algorithms for fluids*, JCP 31, 335–362, https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-9991(79)90051-2 . Motivates simultaneous multidimensional positive/negative capacity limiting. M20 is a local closed-tank D3Q7 flux adaptation, not an exact reproduction of the published scheme.
- Goulding, J., Shinar, T., Schroeder, C. (2025), *Conservative, bounded, and nonlinear discretization of the Cahn-Hilliard-Navier-Stokes equations*, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021999124008805 . Independent evidence of the importance of bound-preserving phase discretizations; different PDE/discretization than M20.
- Fakhari, A., Mitchell, T., Leonardi, C., Bolster, D. (2017), *Improved locality of the phase-field lattice-Boltzmann model for immiscible fluids at high density ratios*, Phys. Rev. E 96, 053301, https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.96.053301 . Direct next physical model candidate; abstract/metadata verified, not implemented. No water–air density-ratio simulation yet.
- Inherited M19 dual-rail circuit based on Khan, Succi and Yao (2026), arXiv:2604.25429; previous milestone audit covers this mechanism. No new quantum gate algorithm claimed.
