# Milestone 14 — D4-covariant reversible collision channel

## Scope and decision
M13 is the frozen baseline for this experiment. Its D2Q25 nonnegative integer shallow-water populations, pressure correction, partial relaxation, streaming and bounce-back are unchanged. M14 adds a three-qubit orientation environment and controlled permutations around the existing reversible collision circuit. The goal is to remove directional bias at the channel level, not to claim a new continuum-isotropic equilibrium, solve dry-front realizability, or simulate a three-dimensional water–air interface. This track is separate from the GitHub two-phase Carleman/VOF production research.

## Exact channel construction
Let D4 be the order-eight symmetry group of the square; it acts on 25 velocity populations by permutations P_g. Let C denote the M13 collision function and U_C its XOR-into-output reversible circuit. Define C_g(f)=P_g^{-1} C(P_g f). A three-qubit register initialized by H^⊗3 prepares the uniform orientation superposition. Controlled rotations by 90 and 180 degrees and reflection generate all eight P_g. Conjugate U_C by the controlled word permutations on both input and output registers (the latter is essential for arbitrary output-register XOR). The reversible dilation acts as

```
|f>|0>|0_scratch>|000> -> 1/sqrt(8) sum_g |f>|C_g(f)>|0_scratch>|g>
```

The overflow flag is also retained in the output (omitted in this formula). The selector is *not* a cleaned scratch register. The conditional permutation uses controlled SWAP = CX, CCX, CX. Reversing the network and then applying H^⊗3 restores the initial state. If orientation is traced out or measured and ignored, the resulting channel is the finite-group average of the conjugated collision channels. Under any group action a, applying a to the input reindexes the eight branches by group multiplication; hence the output channel is covariant for all density matrices, and the conditional population distributions transform equivariantly. This is a group-theoretic identity, not a numerical fit. For a fixed basis input the mean populations are sum_g C_g(f)/8 and may contain fractional ticks. Each branch independently preserves integer nonnegativity, mass and both momentum components (because the original collision preserves the transformed moments and the inverse permutation restores their orientation). A single sampled branch need not be generally equivariant; the full distribution is. Square symmetry does not imply arbitrary-angle continuum isotropy or physical viscosity.

The local coherent verification uses an exact **sparse complex statevector**: the support terms are explicitly expanded under H and the complete X/CX/CCX gate stream is executed on each basis label via the prior bit-sliced gate interpreter. Relative complex amplitudes are retained. This is mathematically exact for these permutation gates and the small support used, not a dense simulation of all 2^141088 possible amplitudes. The grid experiment draws seeded uniform labels to emulate measuring the orientation register, executes the same controlled gate network and uses the previous reversible word transport; there is no classical CFD solver in the update path. Basis states are reconstructed between steps. The independent integer reference checks every active cell at every step; the floating-point projected kinetic solver is a separate comparator.

## Quantum resource accounting
At S=256, omega=1, M13 local collision has 459733 X/CX/CCX gates and 141085 logical qubits. M14 adds 4800 controlled-permutation elementary X/CX/CCX gates, three H gates and three selector qubits, for 464536 gates and 141088 logical qubits (about 1.04% more local gates). At S=256, omega=.5, the base 499497 gates become 504300 including H. These are untranspiled logical counts, not physical-resource costs. A coherently retained selector for each of 241 active cells needs 723 selector qubits per timestep, not just three globally. Reusing those qubits would require tracing/resetting or otherwise retaining environment information; no coherent multi-step resource estimate is inferred from local counts. State preparation and readout costs are not included. No postselection is required for the orientation average, but averaging does not eliminate the original dissipative collision's history register or solve end-to-end state preparation.

## Next scientific gate
The most important unresolved physics limitation is dry-front moment realizability on a fixed finite speed grid, not another pressure rounding tweak. Before another expensive circuit, compare signed population encoding, adaptive/local velocity scale, and a distinct well-balanced positivity-preserving shallow-water/free-surface scheme. Use independent classical screening only, clearly labelled; require convergence against Ritter before quantum compilation, and use a matched reference with fixed physical domain/time and independent precision refinement. For the original water–air research, review the separate production branch and compare a 3D D3Q19 VOF/free-surface reference against appropriate physical benchmarks; do not relabel D2Q25 depth as water–air VOF. In quantum architecture, investigate how to retain or discard collision history in a well-defined channel and verify two coherent timesteps on a small system, then native Qiskit simulation/transpilation and resource accounting. The literature audit in SOURCE_AUDIT.md records current alternative algorithms; no paper's performance is attributed to this code.
