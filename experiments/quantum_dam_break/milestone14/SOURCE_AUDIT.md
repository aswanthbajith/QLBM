## Scientific literature checked
1. Schalkers, M.A. and Möller, M. (2024), *On the importance of data encoding in quantum Boltzmann methods*, Quantum Information Processing. https://doi.org/10.1007/s11128-023-04216-6 . Explicitly analyzes constraints on unitary collision and streaming for common encodings; supports separating basis-word reversible dilation from amplitude-based evolution and accounting for history. M14 does not solve those general encoding constraints.
2. *Random Covariant Quantum Channels*, Annales Henri Poincaré (published online 2025), https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-025-01558-y , section 1.1. Defines channel twirling with conjugation of the input and output representations. M14 applies its finite-group average directly with D4 and a Stinespring orientation environment; implementation and benchmarks here are project-specific. The channel is not sampled from the paper's random-isometry ensemble.
3. Augier, A., Dubois, F., Graille, B. and Lallemand, P. (2014), *On rotational invariance of lattice Boltzmann schemes*, Computers & Mathematics with Applications 67, 239–255. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.camwa.2013.06.009 . Motivates treating isotropy/order of equivalent PDEs as a separate test; finite D4 equivariance is a weaker property than continuum rotational invariance.
4. Bastida Zamora et al. (2026), *Quantum lattice Boltzmann method for several time steps: A local Carleman linearization algorithm*, Physical Review E 113, 035307. https://doi.org/10.1103/kbx3-c35d ; preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.13072 . Publisher metadata/abstract and preprint listing checked; publisher full text not accessible. A current alternative for nonlinear coherent multi-step research; no implementation or reported success probability reproduced in M14.
5. *Quantum lattice Boltzmann method for simulating nonlinear fluid dynamics* (2025), npj Quantum Information 11, 196. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-025-01142-6 . Probability encoding and lattice-gas-derived collision are alternative strategies; the paper discusses encoding and hardware resource challenges. This is not the algorithm implemented in M14.
6. *Surrogate Quantum Circuit Design for the Lattice Boltzmann Collision Operator* (2026), International Journal for Numerical Methods in Engineering. https://doi.org/10.1002/nme.70286 . Abstract checked: learned low-depth surrogate with encoding and measurement choices to approximate nonlinear BGK. Candidate alternative, not adopted here because an independently verified deterministic baseline and conservation tests were already available. No claim of reproducing the paper's accuracy or depth.

The latest-research search also identified 2026 preprints on representation-split Carleman encoding and conserved/relaxing modes (arXiv:2610.20620 and 2609.32310); preprint status and abstract only. They are not treated as established evidence or implemented results. Literature is evidence for selection and limitations, not a substitute for executing code.
